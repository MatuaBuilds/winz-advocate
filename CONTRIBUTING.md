# Help make Village Advocate stronger

This tool gets better when people share what actually happened to them at Work and
Income. You don't need to be technical to help.

## Share what happened to you (most valuable)

If you used the tool, or dealt with WINZ yourself, tell us:

- What you asked for
- What WINZ said (especially anything that turned out to be wrong)
- What worked — the words, the section of the Act, the evidence, the persistence
- What you'd tell the next person in your situation

**How:** open a [new issue](../../issues/new/choose) and pick **"Share what happened"**.
Or email the maintainers (see README).

**Please remove personal details** — no full names, client numbers, addresses, or
dates of birth (yours or a staff member's). We want the lesson, not the identity.

## Suggest a correction

If a figure, rule, or link in `knowledge/` is out of date or wrong, open an issue and
pick **"Suggest a correction"**. Include the correct information and a link to an
official source (workandincome.govt.nz, legislation.govt.nz, or Community Law) if you
have one.

## Report something the tool got wrong

If the tool gave advice that was inaccurate or made someone's situation worse, that's
the most important thing to tell us. Open an issue and pick **"Report something wrong"**.

## How contributions are used

- Shared experiences that check out are de-identified and added to
  `knowledge/community-notes/` as "other people found…" notes — they inform the tool
  but never override the official rules in `knowledge/entitlements/`.
- Corrections are checked against an official source before the knowledge files change.
- Every knowledge file keeps its `## Source` section and "checked" date.

## For advocates and organisations

You're welcome to use, adapt, translate, or embed this. If you maintain a section of
the knowledge base (e.g. a region's food bank list, or a benefit you know deeply),
open an issue or a pull request. The licence is in `LICENSE` (CC BY 4.0 for content).
