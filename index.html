Village Advocate

Free help getting what Work and Income (WINZ) owes you — built for people whose ADHD, AuDHD, or trauma makes the forms, phone calls, and deadlines too much.

It works out what you can apply for, writes the emails for you to send, and helps you push back when a decision is wrong. It is your advocate. You are always the one who hits send.

Use it now

Village Advocate runs inside a free AI assistant. Two steps:

  1. Open a free account at claude.ai (or another assistant you already use).
  2. Start a chat, paste in the instructions then the knowledge pack, and say "I need help with Work and Income."

Full setup, including how to make it a reusable project: SET-UP-THE-CLAUDE-PROJECT.md.

The rules this tool always follows
  • It never sends anything — it writes the draft, you send it.
  • It never invents an entitlement — every claim points to its source.
  • Amounts change (usually each 1 April) — check the current figure with WINZ.
  • If WINZ pursues you for fraud or benefit crime, that's criminal — get a lawyer through Community Law or Legal Aid.
  • Your information stays yours. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere.
