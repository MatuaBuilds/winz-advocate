WINZ Advocate

a tool from The Village

Get what Work and Income owes you.

It works out everything you can apply for, writes the emails and the requests for you, and helps you push back when a decision is wrong. Built for people whose ADHD, AuDHD, or trauma makes the forms, phone calls and deadlines too much. You are always the one who hits send.

Free, community-run, independent. Not Work and Income. Not the government. Nobody is paid, and no one can put a login or a price on it.

Start using it — 3 steps

  1. Open a free account at claude.ai (or another AI assistant you already use) and start a new chat.
  2. Copy the whole tool with this button, and paste it into the chat.
    Button not working? Download it and paste the contents instead.
  3. Send: “I need help with Work and Income.” Then answer its questions, one at a time. Stop whenever — paste your saved notes back next time to pick up where you left off.

Want it as a saved project you don't paste each time? See the setup guide.

What it does for you

Finds your entitlements

Asks about your situation and names every payment you can apply for — with the reason and the source for each. It checks the lot, even the ones WINZ never mentions.

Writes the emails

Ready-to-send messages to your case manager: apply for a grant, book an appointment, chase a delay, dispute a debt.

Helps you push back

When a decision is wrong it drafts your Review of Decision, gives you the legal reason why, and preps you for the hearing.

Keeps it together

A simple case file tracks your deadlines, what you've applied for, and every reply from WINZ.

The rules it always follows

It never sends anything. It writes the draft — you read it and send it.
It never invents an entitlement. Every claim points to its source.
Amounts change (usually each 1 April). It tells you to check the current figure with WINZ.
Fraud is different. If WINZ accuses you of benefit crime, that's criminal — get a lawyer through Community Law or Legal Aid.
Your information stays yours. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere.
