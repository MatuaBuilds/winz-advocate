WINZ Advocate

Get what Work and Income owes you. It works out what you can apply for, writes the emails for you, and helps you push back when a decision is wrong.

Built for people whose ADHD, AuDHD, or trauma makes the forms, phone calls, and deadlines too much. You are always the one who hits send.

Start using it Just read the guide

Start using it — 3 steps

  1. Open a free account at claude.ai (or another AI assistant you already use) and start a new chat.
  2. Copy the whole tool with the button below, and paste it into the chat.
    Not working? Download it instead and paste the contents.
  3. Send this message: "I need help with Work and Income." Then just answer its questions, one at a time. Take breaks whenever — paste your saved notes back in next time to pick up where you left off.

Want it as a permanent project you don't have to paste each time? See the setup guide.

What it does for you

Works out your entitlements

Asks about your situation and tells you every payment you can apply for — with the reason and the source for each.

Writes the emails

Ready-to-send messages to your case manager: apply for a grant, book an appointment, chase a delay.

Helps you push back

When a decision is wrong, it drafts your Review of Decision and preps you for the hearing. It knows the rules better than the front desk.

Keeps it together

A simple case file tracks your deadlines, what you've applied for, and every reply from WINZ.

The rules it always follows

  • It never sends anything. It writes the draft — you read it and send it.
  • It never invents an entitlement. Every claim points to its source.
  • Amounts change (usually each 1 April) — check the current figure with WINZ.
  • Fraud is different. If WINZ accuses you of benefit crime, that's criminal — get a lawyer through Community Law or Legal Aid.
  • Your information stays yours. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere.
