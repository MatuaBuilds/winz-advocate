## Cases

### Housing

- **Status:** Waiting on someone
- **Goal:** Secure stable housing and prepare to relocate.
- **Current state:** WINZ housing paperwork signed; MyMSD upload section open;
  confirmation letter requested from current household; working move-out date ~3
  months away, may shift.
- **Evidence held:** signed WINZ housing paperwork.
- **Waiting on:** confirmation letter from the household.
- **Next action:** chase the letter; upload to MyMSD when it arrives.
- **Notes:** prepare the move in parallel — don't wait for the address.

### Move-in — empty property setup

- **Status:** Back burner (prep only)
- **Goal:** Move into an empty property and make it usable immediately.
- **Current state:** listing essentials (bed, whiteware, seating, kitchen, heating);
  no suppliers researched yet.
- **Next action:** research suppliers — delivery + installation + lead time + total
  delivered cost + MSD preferred-supplier status. See `modules/housing/the-move.md`.
- **Notes:** outcome = delivered, installed, usable.

### Disability Allowance claim

- **Status:** Waiting on someone
- **Goal:** Get an ongoing health cost recognised through WINZ Disability Allowance.
- **Current state:** form obtained; case manager says the gap is *current cost
  evidence + certification the cost is ongoing* — not the diagnosis.
- **Evidence held:** work-capacity medical certificate; GP health summary; specialist
  reports; treatment/approval letter; prescription info.
- **Waiting on:** prescriber (escalated to their medical team); GP.
- **Next action:** none until a provider replies — chase both by 2026-09-08.
- **Notes:** do not rebuild the medical story; the gap is narrow.

### Therapy

- **Status:** Complete / active
- **Goal:** Consistent therapeutic support.
- **Current state:** re-engaged and organised.

### Money — KiwiSaver hardship

- **Status:** Ready to act
- **Goal:** Significant financial hardship withdrawal, toward stability and transport.
- **Next action (bounded):** identify the KiwiSaver provider and get their hardship
  form.
- **Notes:** link the transport need to health, therapy attendance and housing
  stability in the application. A budgeting service can help prepare it.

### Money — one-off travel cost

- **Status:** Back burner
- **Goal:** ~$1,700 for a specific trip.
- **Next action:** none until the front-burner cases move.
- **Notes:** its own target — not merged into "money".

### Support network (pre-move)

- **Status:** Waiting on someone
- **Goal:** Build support before relocating.
- **Current state:** contacted a housing navigator, Community Law, and a health &
  disability advocacy service.
- **Next action:** log replies; keep the useful relationships, drop the rest.
