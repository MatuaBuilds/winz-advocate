## Email to an advocacy org (AAAP, Community Law, beneficiary advocates)

Subject: Free open tool for WINZ entitlements — would value your eyes on it

Kia ora,

I've built a free, open tool called **WINZ Advocate**. It acts like a benefit
advocate: guided intake, works out what someone's entitled to (with sources), drafts
the emails and Review of Decision requests, and coaches people to push back on wrong
decisions and use the discretion provisions. It's aimed at people whose ADHD, AuDHD,
or trauma makes the system's load a barrier.

It's not a service I run — it's a knowledge base plus instructions that anyone can use
in a free AI assistant, and it's openly licensed (CC BY 4.0) so you can use, adapt,
translate, or build on it.

- Try it / read the guide: https://matuabuilds.github.io/winz-advocate/
- The knowledge and code: https://github.com/MatuaBuilds/winz-advocate

Two asks:
1. Would you look at the entitlements and process pages and tell me anything that's
   wrong or out of date? (There's a one-click "suggest a correction" button.)
2. If it's useful, share it with the people you can't get to.

Everything it says points people to you for anything high-stakes, and it never sends
anything on anyone's behalf.

Ngā mihi,
Bill

## When you share it, say what it is honestly

- It's a **self-help tool**, not a person and not a service with a waitlist.
- It uses **real WINZ information** but amounts change — it tells people to check.
- It **never sends anything** — the person always reads and sends themselves.
- For **fraud allegations or prosecution**, people still need a lawyer.
