# Planning prompt (kept for reference)

This is the prompt used to start the project. You do not need to run it again —
`SPEC.md` and `PLAN.md` are the result. Keep it in case you want to re-plan from
scratch later, or start a related project.

---

You are helping me design Version 1 of a free tool (working name: "Village Advocate").

MISSION
It does what a New Zealand welfare/benefit advocate does — helps people who are
entitled to support from Work and Income (WINZ/MSD) actually get it — for people
whose ADHD, AuDHD, or trauma makes the system's forms, phone calls, deadlines and
executive-function load a serious barrier. The users are individuals helping
themselves. The goal is the most useful FREE advocate tool for people with the
least resources.

THE JOB TO BE DONE (what a WINZ advocate does)
1. Intake — full picture: income, rent/housing, health & disability, dependents,
   relationship status, debts, immigration status, current benefits.
2. Entitlement check — identify every payment, grant and service the person
   qualifies for but isn't getting (main benefit, Accommodation Supplement,
   Disability Allowance, Temporary Additional Support, Special Needs Grants
   incl. food, Advance Payments, Childcare Assistance, and more).
3. Prepare — list evidence/documents needed, help complete forms, draft cover
   letters, coordinate medical certificates.
4. Communicate — draft emails to case managers and WINZ: request appointments,
   follow up, chase delays, push a stalled application forward. Plain, firm,
   correct language. Track the whole correspondence thread.
5. Challenge — when a decision is wrong: draft a Review of Decision within the
   3-month window, and know the escalation path (Benefit Review Committee →
   Social Security Appeal Authority → Ombudsman).
6. Track — every deadline, review date, obligation and stand-down.
7. Food security — advocate with food-parcel services and make referrals.

NON-NEGOTIABLES
- A finished, usable Version 1 beats a clever automated system that never ships.
- The tool NEVER sends an email or submits anything itself. It drafts; a human sends.
- Advocacy support, not legal advice. Hand off to real advocates (AAAP, Community Law)
  for reviews, appeals, and anything high-stakes.
- Entitlement knowledge must be MAINTAINABLE and CITED. Never invent a rule.
- Data is extremely sensitive. Privacy-first. The person owns their data.
- ADHD/AuDHD/trauma-aware: one question at a time, resumable, max three next actions.

DELIVERABLES: interview me with AskUserQuestion on the hard parts, recommend the
build target, then write SPEC.md and PLAN.md (Phase 1 = walking skeleton).
