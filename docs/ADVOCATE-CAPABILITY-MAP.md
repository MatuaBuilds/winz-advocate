# What a WINZ advocate does — capability map

Everything a free human advocate (AAAP, Community Law, beneficiary advocacy services)
handles, and where WINZ Advocate stands against it.

Sources: AAAP Empowerment Booklet, Community Law Manual (chapters on dealing with
Work and Income; penalties, investigations and overpayments), Work and Income.

Status key: **✅ covered** · **◑ partial/thin** · **✗ missing**
