# Test WINZ Advocate yourself — step by step

No technical anything. About 10 minutes.

## Load it up

1. On your phone or computer, go to **claude.ai** and sign in (make a free account if
   you don't have one).
2. Start a **new chat**.
3. In another tab, go to **https://matuabuilds.github.io/winz-advocate/**
4. Click the orange **"Copy the tool"** button. It should say "Copied."
5. Go back to the Claude chat, tap the message box, **paste**, and send it.
   (It's long — that's meant to happen. It's the whole tool.)
6. Now send one more message: **`I need help with Work and Income`**

That's it. It's running.

## Try it out

Pretend to be someone who needs help. Give it a situation and see what it does.
Some to try:

- *"I'm 30, on Jobseeker, renting a room for $250 a week, and I've got no money for
  food this week."*
- *"I'm a single mum, just left my partner, one kid aged 3, no income yet."*
- *"WINZ declined my Supported Living Payment and I think they're wrong."*
- *"WINZ sent me a letter saying I owe them $2,000."*
- *"I've been on a benefit for 8 years, I'm pretty sure I get everything I can."*

## What good looks like

- **Calm, one question at a time** — not a wall of questions.
- **Deals with anything urgent first** (food, power, nowhere to stay).
- **Names real payments** with a reason for each — and checks the ones people miss.
- **Gives you 3 things to do**, in order.
- **Offers to write the email** for you, with blank spots `[like this]` for your details.
- **Never says it will send anything** — you send it.
- If you mention **fraud** — it should tell you to get a lawyer, not try to handle it.
- Every dollar amount comes with "check the current figure".

## If something's off

Just tell me (Claude), in plain words:

> "When I said [what you typed], it did [what happened]. That's wrong because [why]."

I'll fix the tool, test it, and push the fix live. You don't do anything else.

## Testing with a real person

When you're ready: sit **beside** someone who's actually dealing with WINZ (with
their okay), and let them use it while you watch. Note where they get confused, and
anything it says that turns out wrong. Tell me. That feedback is the most valuable
thing there is — it goes in `docs/OUTCOMES.md`.
