# Contacting Work and Income / your case manager

## The main ways to make contact

| Channel | Best for |
|---|---|
| **MyMSD** (my.msd.govt.nz or the app) | Applying for help, uploading documents, reporting changes, seeing payments and letters, re-applying |
| **Phone 0800 559 009** (over 65: 0800 552 002) | Urgent food/rent, booking or changing an appointment, questions a case manager needs to answer. Open Mon–Fri, roughly 7am–6pm, and Sat 8am–1pm (check current hours) |
| **In person at a service centre** | Identity checks, when phone/online isn't working for you |
| **Email / letter** | A written record of a request or a complaint |

MyMSD does not have reliable direct case-manager messaging. For anything that needs a
person, phone is usually faster, and it creates a note on your file.

## What MyMSD lets you do

- Apply for a benefit, food grant, emergency dental, school costs
- Re-apply for Jobseeker Support, Sole Parent Support, Temporary Additional Support
- Upload documents
- Report changes: income, address, rent, relationship, travel
- See payment history, debts and repayment schedules, and retrieve some letters

## Appointments

- Book, change, or cancel by phoning **0800 559 009**.
- You can ask for a **phone appointment** instead of in person — say so when booking,
  and again if they push back. Illness, disability, anxiety, transport, and caregiving
  are all valid reasons.
- If you can't make an appointment, **phone before it** — a missed appointment can
  affect payments. They can do it by phone or rebook.

## Your rights when dealing with Work and Income

- You can bring a **support person, whānau member, or advocate** to any appointment or
  phone call. You don't need permission. If a staff member objects, ask for that in
  writing and contact a free advocacy service.
- You can ask for any decision, and the **reasons for it, in writing**.
- You can ask to **see the information on your file** about you.
- If you think a decision is wrong, you can ask for a **Review of Decision** — there
  is a time limit (generally 3 months), so act quickly. Village Advocate can help you
  lodge it (see `review-of-decision.md`); free human backup is also available
  (`../support-network.md`).
- Staff must treat you with respect. If they don't, you can make a complaint and/or
  ask to deal with a different case manager.

## Practical tips

- Write down the **date, time, and name** of anyone you speak to, and what was said.
- Ask for a **reference number** for each request.
- Put important requests in writing (through MyMSD or email) so there's a record.
- Be specific: name the exact payment you're asking about.
- If you're told "no", ask **which rule** that's based on and for it **in writing**.
- Stay calm and factual on the phone even when it's hard — it keeps the focus on your entitlement.
