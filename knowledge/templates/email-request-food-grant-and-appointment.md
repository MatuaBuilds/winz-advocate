# Template — request a food grant and an appointment

**When to use:** you have no money for food this week, and you also want an
appointment to sort out other help (Accommodation Supplement, Disability Allowance,
etc.).

**How to send:** through MyMSD (message/contact), or by email to your service centre,
or read it out when you phone **0800 559 009**. Keep a copy.

**Fill in every [square bracket]. Delete this instruction block before sending.**

---

Subject: Urgent food assistance request and appointment request — [Full name], client number [client number]

I have no money available for food and no other way to pay for it. [One line on why —

for a Special Needs Grant for food.

My situation:
- Income: [what you get and how much per week]
- Money available in my accounts: [$amount], which is committed to [rent / power / debt repayment] and not available for food
- Household: [e.g. single adult / me and [number] children aged [ages]]

Please let me know what you need from me to process this today. I can provide recent
bank statements.

- [other]
