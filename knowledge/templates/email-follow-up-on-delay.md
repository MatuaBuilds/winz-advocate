# Template — follow up on a delay

**When to use:** you applied for something (a grant, a benefit, a change) and haven't
heard back, or it's taking longer than you were told.

**How to send:** through MyMSD, or by email to your service centre. Keep a copy.

**Fill in every [square bracket]. Delete this instruction block before sending.**

---

Subject: Follow-up on [what you applied for] — [Full name], client number [client number]

Kia ora,

My name is [Full name], client number [client number].

On [date] I applied for [what you applied for] [reference number, if you have one].
I was told to expect a decision by [date / timeframe you were given, or "and have not
yet had a response"].

I have not had a decision or any update. This is causing [short, factual impact —
e.g. "I cannot cover this week's food and power" / "I am falling behind on rent"].

Please:
1. Let me know the current status of this application.
2. Tell me if anything further is needed from me.
3. Give me a date by which I can expect a decision.

If a decision has been made, please send it to me **in writing with the reasons**.

Ngā mihi,
[Full name]
[Phone number]
[Email]
