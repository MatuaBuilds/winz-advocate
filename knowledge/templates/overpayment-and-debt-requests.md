# Templates — overpayment and debt

Three requests, in the order you usually send them. See
`../process/overpayments-and-debt.md` for the background and
`../precedents.md` for the write-off test.

**Fill in every [square bracket]. Delete the instruction lines before sending.**
Send through MyMSD or by email to your service centre. Keep a copy and note the date.

---

## 1. Ask for the full breakdown (send first)

Subject: Request for breakdown of alleged overpayment — [Full name], client number [number]

Kia ora,

I have received a letter dated [date] saying I was overpaid [$amount] for the period
[dates].

Before anything is deducted or agreed, please send me **in writing**:
- how the overpayment was calculated (the figures used, week by week)
- what income or change it is based on, and the source of that information
- the exact period it covers
- which rule or policy it relies on

Please **pause any deductions** until I have had a chance to check this. I may dispute
the overpayment or its amount.

Ngā mihi,
[Full name] · [phone] · [email] · [date]

---

## 2. Ask for the debt to be written off (if it was WINZ's error)

Subject: Request to write off overpayment — [Full name], client number [number]

Kia ora,

I am asking for the overpayment of [$amount] (letter dated [date], period [dates]) to
be **written off**. All three grounds apply:

1. **It was caused by an error by Work and Income.** [Explain — e.g. "I reported my
   income on [date] through MyMSD / by phone to [name]. The overpayment happened
   because Work and Income did not apply that correctly." Attach proof if you have it.]

2. **I received the payments in good faith.** I had no reason to think they were
   wrong. The amounts looked normal and I was not told otherwise.

3. **I changed my position in reliance on them, and recovery would be inequitable.**
   I budgeted and spent on the basis of that income [rent, power, food, essentials for
   my children]. I cannot repay it now without serious hardship.

Please confirm the write-off in writing. If it is declined, I would like the decision
and the reasons in writing, as I intend to apply for a **Review of Decision**.

Ngā mihi,
[Full name] · [phone] · [email] · [date]

---

## 3. Ask for repayments to be reduced (if you do have to repay)

Subject: Request to reduce weekly deductions — [Full name], client number [number]

Kia ora,

Deductions of [$amount] a week are currently coming off my payments for [debt / debts].

After rent, power, and other essential costs, this leaves me unable to afford enough
food [and other essentials for my children]. I am asking for the total weekly
deductions to be **reduced to [$amount] a week** [or "to the minimum available"].

I understand the debt will take longer to repay. I need the weekly amount to be
manageable so my family and I can meet basic costs.

Please confirm the new deduction amount in writing.

Ngā mihi,
[Full name] · [phone] · [email] · [date]
