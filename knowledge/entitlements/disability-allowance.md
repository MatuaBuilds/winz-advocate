# Disability Allowance

## What it is

A weekly payment (not taxed, never repaid) for people who have **regular, ongoing
extra costs because of a disability or health condition** — things like doctor's
visits, prescriptions, travel to appointments, extra heating, counselling, special
food, or a medical alarm.

"Disability" here is broad. It includes long-term physical illness, mental health
conditions, and neurodevelopmental conditions where they create ongoing costs and
are expected to last **6 months or more**. ADHD, autism, and the effects of trauma
can qualify where there are real, regular, verified costs.

## Who can get it

You may be eligible if you:

- have a disability or health condition expected to last **at least 6 months**
- have **regular ongoing costs** because of it that aren't fully covered by another
  agency (e.g. Pharmac, ACC)
- are a New Zealand citizen or permanent resident, normally living in NZ
- are under the income limit for your situation. From 1 April 2026, before tax:
  single 18+ **$870.00/week**, sole parent with 1 child **$971.51/week**, sole parent
  with 2+ children **$1,023.59/week** (couple limits are higher — check the current
  table). You can be working and still qualify.

You do not have to be on a benefit.

## How much

Up to **$82.85 a week** — but only up to the actual amount of your verified costs.
If your extra costs add up to $40/week, you get $40/week.

## What costs can be claimed

Examples: doctor and specialist fees, prescription charges, ongoing counselling or
therapy, travel to medical appointments, extra power/heating a health condition
requires, gluten-free or other medically-required special food, medical alarms,
continence products, gym membership where a health provider says it's needed.

Each cost needs to be **regular and ongoing** and **confirmed as necessary by a
health practitioner**.

## How to apply

1. Fill in the **Disability Allowance application form**.
2. Get a **Disability Certificate** completed by your doctor or nurse practitioner —
   this confirms the condition, that it lasts 6+ months, and the costs it causes.
3. Gather proof of each cost — receipts, a pharmacy printout, a quote, an invoice,
   or an appointment schedule with fees.
4. Submit through **MyMSD** or at a service centre. Phone **0800 559 009**.

## Common reasons it's declined or underpaid

- The Disability Certificate is vague — ask the doctor to list each specific cost
- Costs claimed as one-off rather than regular/ongoing
- No receipts or proof of the amount
- A cost that another agency should cover (claim it there first, then any gap here)
- Not reapplying — the allowance is usually granted for a set period and must be renewed

## Tips

- Book a longer GP appointment so the doctor can fill the certificate properly.
- Keep a simple list of every health-related cost for a month before applying.
- If a cost changes (e.g. a new regular prescription), tell Work and Income — the
  payment can be increased up to the maximum.
