# Working for Families (and Best Start)

Payments for families with dependent children. **Inland Revenue (IRD) administers
these, not Work and Income** — but an advocate should still explain them and make
sure the person is registered, because it's real money that's easy to miss,
especially just after a separation or a new baby.

You can get Working for Families whether you're **on a benefit or working**. Register
through **IRD (myIR)** — or, if you're applying for a benefit, Work and Income can
start it for you.

## The four payments

| Payment | For | Guide amount (confirm current with IRD) |
|---|---|---|
| **Family Tax Credit** | The main one — any family with dependent children, on a benefit or low-to-middle income | Varies by number of children and income; often the largest WFF payment |
| **Best Start** | Every family with a child **under 1** (not income tested in year 1); **age 1–2** income tested | About **$77/week** for children born on/after 1 April 2026; reduces once family income is over ~$79,000 |
| **In-Work Tax Credit** | Families **not on a benefit** who work (broadly, a couple 30 hrs/wk or a sole parent 20 hrs/wk) | Up to **$147/week** from 1 April 2026 |
| **Minimum Family Tax Credit** | Working families **not on a benefit** whose income is below a set floor — tops them up to it | Tops after-tax income up to the guaranteed minimum |

## Common issues an advocate helps with

- **Not registered at all** — a new sole parent often isn't. Register with IRD now;
  it can be **backdated** in some cases.
- **Getting it weekly vs as a lump sum** — weekly helps cash flow; ask IRD to switch.
- **Income estimate wrong** — if your estimate is too high you're underpaid all year
  (you get it back at square-up); too low and you get a bill. Update your estimate
  when income changes.
- **Moving on/off a benefit** — the payment type changes (In-Work Tax Credit stops on
  a benefit; Family Tax Credit continues). Tell IRD when your situation changes so
  you're paid the right one.
- **Shared care** — WFF can be split; make sure the split matches actual care.
- **Child support** received can affect Sole Parent Support but **not** WFF.

## How to apply

- **myIR** at ird.govt.nz, or phone IRD **0800 227 773**.
- Use the estimator: ird.govt.nz/tools-calculators
- If applying for a benefit, ask Work and Income to start your WFF at the same time.
