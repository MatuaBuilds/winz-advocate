Not fully built. This is about **tracking relationships with services**, not a
directory.

## The rule

Don't just hand someone a list of services. For every service contacted, the case
tracks:

| Field | |
|---|---|
| Service | name |
| Contacted | date, how (phone/email/in person) |
| What was asked | the specific ask |
| Replied | date, or still waiting |
| Can actually help with | what they confirmed they do |
| Useful? | yes / no / partly |
| Next action | with them, or move on |

A service that was contacted and hasn't replied is a `Waiting on someone` item with a
chase date — not an open task on the person.

## Common free services (NZ)

- **Community Law** — free legal help: https://communitylaw.org.nz
- **Citizens Advice Bureau** — info + referrals: 0800 367 222
- **MoneyTalks** — free budgeting: 0800 345 123
- **Health and Disability Advocacy** — free: 0800 555 050
- **Auckland Action Against Poverty** — WINZ advocacy: advocates@aaap.org.nz
- Regional housing navigators / iwi services (e.g. Te Pokapū in some areas)
- **1737** — free call/text counselling, any time
