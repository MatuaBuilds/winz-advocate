# Module: Money & debt  (STUB)

Not fully built. Enough to route and point to the right help.

## What this covers

Financial stability beyond weekly income — KiwiSaver hardship withdrawals, debt,
budgeting, and one-off funding targets. (Weekly benefit income, WINZ debt, and WINZ
hardship grants are the WINZ module — route there.)

## Key rule

**Don't turn every financial problem into one big "money" issue.** Break it into
separate cases, each with its own funding target and its own path:

- "significant financial hardship KiwiSaver withdrawal" — one case
- "a specific one-off cost" (e.g. flights) — a separate case with its own $ target
- "reduce weekly deductions / dispute a WINZ debt" — WINZ module

## KiwiSaver — significant financial hardship withdrawal

- You apply through **your KiwiSaver provider** (not IRD, not WINZ). First step:
  identify the provider and get their hardship withdrawal form.
- The provider's supervisor decides. You generally must show you can't meet **minimum
  living expenses**, or specific hardship (mortgage/rent arrears, medical costs,
  funeral costs, etc.).
- Evidence usually needed: income and expenses, debts, bank statements, arrears
  letters, and a written explanation of the hardship and why the withdrawal helps.
- **If a car / reliable transport is the goal**, the case is stronger when you link
  it to health, therapy attendance, housing stability, and getting to appointments —
  spell that connection out.
- A budgeting service (below) can help prepare the application and is often expected.
