# Module: Health admin  (STUB)

Not fully built. Enough to route and point to the right help.

## What this covers

The paperwork side of health — coordinating medical evidence, GP and specialist
letters, managing certificates, and getting health costs recognised by other systems.
(The **money** for health costs via Work and Income — Disability Allowance,
prescriptions, emergency dental/medical — is the WINZ module. ACC is its own module,
not started.)

## Key points

- **Build the medical evidence file once, reuse it everywhere.** A person's medical
  story usually already exists across: a Work Capacity / medical certificate, GP
  health summary, specialist letters, ACC reports, treatment/approval letters. List
  these in the case's **evidence held**. Before asking a provider for anything new,
  check what's already there.
- **The usual missing piece for a cost claim is not the diagnosis — it's the current
  cost evidence and current certification that the cost is ongoing.** Ask the
  provider specifically for that, not for the whole history again.
- **Requests to providers are `Waiting on someone`.** Track: what was asked, of whom,
  when, and a chase date. If a provider says it's been "escalated to their medical
  team", that's a real waiting state — note it and set a follow-up.
- **Coordinate, don't duplicate.** If two providers can each supply part of what's
  needed (e.g. the GP certifies the condition, the prescriber certifies the cost),
  make that split explicit in the requests.
