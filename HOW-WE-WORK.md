# How we work on this

You've never built software. You don't need to. This is the structure. It doesn't change.

---

## The split

**Your job:**
- The vision. What it should do, who it's for, why it matters.
- Direction. "More of this", "not that", "try this instead".
- Yes / no. Nothing goes live without you saying go.
- Testing with real people.
- Telling me the moment something feels off.

**My job (everything with a keyboard):**
- Research, writing, the knowledge base.
- All the files. All the git. All the pushing and deploying.
- Testing every change against the eval personas before it counts as done.
- Telling you, in plain words, what changed and what's live.

**You will never type a command. If I ask you to, that's my mistake — tell me.**

---

## The loop (every session)

1. **You say what you want.** Rough is fine. A voice ramble is fine. Half an idea is fine.
2. **I say it back** — what I think you mean, and what I'd do about it. A short plan.
3. **You say "go"** — or change it.
4. **I build it**, test it, save it, push it live.
5. **I tell you** what changed and where to see it.
6. **You look** when you want — I give you a link or a screenshot.

Stop any time. Everything is saved automatically. Coming back tomorrow costs nothing.

---

## The rules (from Anthropic's own guidance on working with Claude)

- **Explore, then plan, then build.** We don't jump straight to making things. We look
  first, agree the plan, then do it.
- **Be specific about what matters.** The clearer you are about the parts you care
  about, the less back-and-forth. If I'm unsure, I ask.
- **Every change gets checked.** The eval personas (`docs/EVALS/`) are how we know a
  change made it better and didn't break something else.
- **Course-correct early.** The second I go the wrong way, say so. We back up. Nothing
  is lost.
- **One thing at a time.** We finish a thing before starting the next. Mixing tasks
  makes a mess.

---

## What "it's live" means

- The **repo** (github.com/MatuaBuilds/winz-advocate) — the permanent home of everything.
- The **website** (matuabuilds.github.io/winz-advocate) — what people use.
- When I say "pushed" / "live", it means it's on both. You don't do anything for that
  to happen — I do it.

---

## When you're lost

Say "I'm lost" or "explain that like I've never seen a computer." That's always a
valid move. I'll stop and explain in plain words, or just do the thing and show you
the result.
