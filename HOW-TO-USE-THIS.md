# How to use Village Advocate

This is a helper for getting what Work and Income (WINZ) owes you. It works out what
you can apply for, and writes the emails for you. **You** send them.

## If you only read one thing

Open the tool and type: **"I need help with Work and Income."**
Then just answer its questions, one at a time. Take breaks whenever you want.

## What it will do

1. Ask if anything is urgent this week (food, power, rent).
2. Ask some simple questions about your situation — one at a time.
3. Tell you what you might be entitled to, and why.
4. Give you **three things to do**, in order.
5. Write the emails or messages for you to send to WINZ.
6. Give you a "case file" to copy and keep.

## Keep your case file

At the end it gives you a block of text — your **case file**. Copy it somewhere safe
(a note on your phone, a document). Next time, **paste it back in first** so the tool
picks up where you left off.

## When you get a letter or email back from WINZ

Paste it in and say "this came back from WINZ." The tool will tell you what it means
and what to do next.

## Important

- The tool **never sends anything** — it writes the draft, you read it and send it.
- It uses real WINZ information but **amounts change** — check the dollar figures on
  the WINZ website or by phoning **0800 559 009**.
- It is **help with advocacy, not legal advice**.
- If a decision goes against you and you want to fight it, there is a **3-month time
  limit** — contact a free advocate straight away:
  - Auckland Action Against Poverty — advocates@aaap.org.nz — aaap.org.nz
  - Community Law — communitylaw.org.nz
  - Citizens Advice Bureau — 0800 367 222

## If you're not coping

Free call or text **1737** any time to talk to a trained counsellor. In an emergency, **111**.
