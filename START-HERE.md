## What this is

**WINZ Advocate** — a free, independent tool that helps people get what Work and
Income (WINZ) owes them. Built for people whose ADHD, AuDHD, or trauma makes the
forms, phone calls, and deadlines too much. It works out what you're entitled to,
writes the emails, and helps you push back. Not WINZ. Not the government.

- **Live website:** https://matuabuilds.github.io/winz-advocate/
- **The code:** https://github.com/MatuaBuilds/winz-advocate

## Rules this tool always follows

- It never sends anything itself. It writes the draft; the person sends it.
- It never invents an entitlement. Every claim points to its source.
- Advocacy, not legal advice. Fraud/criminal matters need a lawyer.
- People's information stays theirs.
